## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Unit summaries
The system SHALL generate, for each reading unit of a ready edition, a short summary of what happens in that unit. A summary SHALL be stored with its reading unit's ordinal, label, and end position, so that it can be limited by the same position bound that limits retrieval. A summary SHALL be written from the unit's own text and SHALL NOT use knowledge of later units.

#### Scenario: Summary stored per unit
- **WHEN** preprocessing completes for an edition with 30 reading units
- **THEN** each unit has one current summary stored with that unit's end position

#### Scenario: No later knowledge
- **WHEN** the summary of Chapter 3 is generated
- **THEN** the model receives only the text of Chapter 3 (and, at most, earlier summaries), never text of later units

### Requirement: Position-tagged observations
The system SHALL extract, for each reading unit, observations about characters, places, objects, and motifs: a name, a kind, and a short note of what appears or happens to it in that unit. Each observation SHALL be tagged with the unit's end position. The system SHALL NOT store whole-book profiles or book-level themes. A profile of a subject SHALL be built at query time from observations at or before a bound.

#### Scenario: Observations tagged
- **WHEN** a unit is preprocessed
- **THEN** each extracted observation carries that unit's ordinal and end position

#### Scenario: Profile cannot leak
- **WHEN** a profile of a character is requested with a bound at the end of Chapter 10
- **THEN** it is assembled only from observations whose position is at or before that bound

### Requirement: Bounded access
Every read of derived data SHALL take a position bound, and SHALL return only summaries and observations whose unit end position is at or before it. A bound of none SHALL return all current rows. Stale rows SHALL NOT be returned. A unit whose end position is after the bound SHALL NOT be returned even when the bound falls inside it.

#### Scenario: Bounded summary list
- **WHEN** summaries are read with a bound at the end of Chapter 22
- **THEN** summaries of Chapters 1 to 22 are returned and none after

#### Scenario: Bound inside a unit
- **WHEN** a bound falls in the middle of Chapter 22
- **THEN** the summary and observations of Chapter 22 are not returned

#### Scenario: Stale rows hidden
- **WHEN** a unit's rows are stale
- **THEN** they are not returned to any reader

### Requirement: Grounding and verification
Each summary and observation SHALL cite supporting passages by locator with an exact quote. The system SHALL verify each citation against stored passage text using the existing quote verification, and SHALL store the result. Items with unverified citations SHALL be kept and marked unverified. Citations SHALL be restricted to passages of the unit being processed.

#### Scenario: Verified citation
- **WHEN** an observation cites a quote that appears in the cited passage
- **THEN** it is stored as verified

#### Scenario: Fabricated quote
- **WHEN** an observation cites a quote that does not appear in the cited passage
- **THEN** it is kept and marked unverified

#### Scenario: Citation outside the unit
- **WHEN** an observation cites a locator outside the unit being processed
- **THEN** the citation is stored as unverified

### Requirement: Provenance and staleness
Every derived row SHALL record its edition, reading unit, model, prompt version, and parser version. A reparse of the edition SHALL mark its derived rows stale in the same transaction that replaces the structure. A row SHALL also be treated as stale when its prompt version differs from the current one. Stale rows SHALL be regenerated by the next preprocessing run. Rows SHALL be deleted when the edition is deleted. Canonical text SHALL NOT be modified.

#### Scenario: Reparse invalidates
- **WHEN** an edition is reparsed
- **THEN** its summaries and observations are marked stale and are not used by conversations

#### Scenario: Regeneration
- **WHEN** preprocessing runs for an edition with stale rows
- **THEN** it regenerates every unit that has no current row, and leaves current units untouched

#### Scenario: Edition deleted
- **WHEN** an edition is deleted
- **THEN** its derived rows are deleted with it

### Requirement: Preprocessing job
Preprocessing SHALL run as an `analysis.preprocess` background job, one per edition and deduplicated by edition. The job SHALL split work per reading unit, SHALL skip units that already have a current summary and observations, and SHALL record each unit's result as it completes, so that a retry repeats only unfinished units. A failed unit SHALL NOT discard completed units. Handlers SHALL be idempotent. When no AI provider is configured the system SHALL NOT enqueue or run the job.

#### Scenario: Retry after partial failure
- **WHEN** a job fails on unit 17 of 30 and is retried
- **THEN** units 1 to 16 are not processed again and processing resumes at unit 17

#### Scenario: Duplicate request
- **WHEN** preprocessing is requested twice for one edition while a job is pending or running
- **THEN** only one job exists

#### Scenario: AI not configured
- **WHEN** no AI provider is configured and preprocessing is requested
- **THEN** no job is enqueued and the request reports that AI is not configured

### Requirement: Triggers
When automatic preprocessing is enabled, the system SHALL enqueue the job at the end of a successful ingest and of a successful reparse. Independently of that setting, a reader SHALL be able to start or restart preprocessing for an edition from its detail page. Automatic preprocessing SHALL default to off.

#### Scenario: Automatic on ingest
- **WHEN** automatic preprocessing is enabled and a book finishes ingest
- **THEN** a preprocessing job is enqueued for it

#### Scenario: Automatic off
- **WHEN** automatic preprocessing is off and a book finishes ingest
- **THEN** no job is enqueued until a reader requests it

### Requirement: Status
The system SHALL expose, per edition, preprocessing status (not started, queued, running, complete, partially failed, or stale), the number of units done out of total, the model used, and the last error. Status SHALL never include credentials.

#### Scenario: Progress
- **WHEN** 12 of 30 units are done and the job is running
- **THEN** status reports running with 12 of 30

#### Scenario: Stale after reparse
- **WHEN** an edition was preprocessed and then reparsed
- **THEN** status reports stale until regenerated

### Requirement: Derived data is guidance, not evidence
Summaries and observations SHALL be presented to the assistant as a guide that may be incomplete or wrong. Whole-book synthesis such as book-level themes SHALL NOT be generated.

#### Scenario: No book-level themes
- **WHEN** preprocessing completes
- **THEN** no row describes the book as a whole
