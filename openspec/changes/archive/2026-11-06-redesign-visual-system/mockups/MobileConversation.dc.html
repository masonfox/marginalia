THROUGH YEAR OF GLAD
While competent, Hal is also unreliable, and at times incoherent. Is that the point?

Researched · 160 passages read

Partly. The ending isn’t incoherent on the page. Only the outside view of Hal is.

  • Hal opens with “I am in here” and hedges: “I believe I appear neutral”.
  • He reports speaking “slowly. Distinctly”; the Deans hear “subanimalistic noises”.

So the gap between those two views is the subject, more than Hal’s reliability.
